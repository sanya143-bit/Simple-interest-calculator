# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple web application/program to calculate the simple interest based on the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

## Formula
Simple Interest (SI) is calculated using the formula:
$$\text{SI} = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}$$

Where:
* **P** = Principal Amount
* **R** = Annual Rate of Interest (in %)
* **T** = Time Period (in years)

## Features
* Calculates total interest accurately.
* Shows the total amount (Principal + Interest).
* Simple and user-friendly interface.

## How to Use
1. Enter the Principal amount ($P$).
2. Enter the Rate of interest ($R$).
3. Enter the Time ($T$) in years.
4. Click on the calculate button to get the result.
